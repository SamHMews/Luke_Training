THREE-WEEK WORKOUT PLAN
=======================
3 training days per week | Light -> Medium -> Heavy
Only the main tracked exercises are listed; additional exercises are not included.
Rep ranges are suggested targets based on previous workout logs.

WEEK 1 - LIGHT
--------------
DAY 1 - LEGS & MUSCLE-UPS
Exercise                                  Sets    Target reps
Leg press - full range                    4       90-100
Muscle-ups                                4       6-10

DAY 2 - PUSH & PULL
Bench press                               3       12-15
Wide-grip lat pulldowns                   3       25-30

DAY 3 - SHOULDERS & ARMS
Dumbbell shoulder press                   3       20-25
Lateral raises                            3       45-50
Bent-over lateral raises                  3       35-40

WEEK 2 - MEDIUM
---------------
DAY 1 - LEGS & MUSCLE-UPS
Exercise                                  Sets    Target reps
Leg press - bottom pulses                 4       40-45
Full-range reps after pulses              3       8-10
Leg press - drop set                      1       50 + 100
Muscle-ups                                4-5     5-8

DAY 2 - PUSH & PULL
Bench press                               3       7-10
Pull-ups                                  3       20-25

DAY 3 - SHOULDERS & ARMS
Dumbbell shoulder press                   3       12-16
Lateral raises                            3       40-45
Bent-over flyes                           3       30-35

WEEK 3 - HEAVY
--------------
DAY 1 - LEGS & MUSCLE-UPS
Exercise                                  Sets    Target reps
Leg press - bottom pulses                 4       30-35
Leg press - drop set                      1       50 + 100
Muscle-ups                                4       5-8

DAY 2 - PUSH & PULL
Bench press                               3       1-3
Weighted pull-ups                         3       6-10

DAY 3 - SHOULDERS & ARMS
Standing dumbbell shoulder press          3       8-12
Lateral raises                            3       30-40
Bent-over lateral raises                  3       25-30

PROGRESSION NOTES
-----------------
- Aim for the top of each rep range across all sets before increasing weight,
  while maintaining consistent technique and range of motion.
- High-rep leg presses and lateral raises reflect your existing training style.
- Heavy bench press (1-3 reps) is strength-focused; use appropriate safety measures.
- Leg press full-range reps and drop sets are recorded as components of the
  leg press work, not necessarily separate exercises.
